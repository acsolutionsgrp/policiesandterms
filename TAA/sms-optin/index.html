How verbal opt-in works

Step 1 · Staff reads this script at service intake
“Would you like to receive text message updates from TAA Logistics Inc about your trailer or equipment repair, such as repair status, estimates, and pickup notices? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help. Our Terms are at policiesandterms.com/TAA/terms/ and our Privacy Policy is at policiesandterms.com/TAA/privacy-policy/. Consent is not a condition of receiving service. Do you agree to receive these text messages?”
Step 2 · Customer answers
“Yes.” Staff records the consenting phone number, date, and staff member in the work order or customer record.
Step 3 · TAA Logistics sends enrollment confirmation
