One I.T. Pro ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our clients and contacts. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information, including information collected via SMS/text and RCS messaging as part of our managed IT, network, and support services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, business name, address, phone number, and email address
  • Mobile phone numbers for SMS and RCS communication purposes
  • Support ticket records, service requests, and scheduled appointment history
  • Device, network, and system information necessary to deliver IT support services
  • Communication records including SMS, RCS, and email message history

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Send support ticket status updates and resolution notifications
  • Schedule and confirm on-site and remote service appointments
  • Deliver service outage, maintenance window, and incident alerts
  • Respond to support requests and service inquiries
  • Provide and improve managed IT, network, and cybersecurity services
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

3. SMS and RCS Messaging

By providing your mobile number during service onboarding, by submitting a support request, or by texting One I.T. Pro directly, you consent to receive SMS and RCS messages related to your IT services. Message frequency varies based on your open support tickets, scheduled appointments, and service activity.

RCS (Rich Communication Services) messages are delivered through your device's native messaging application and display our verified business name and logo. Where RCS is not supported by your device or carrier, messages are delivered as standard SMS or MMS.

Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP. Message frequency varies.

4. No Mobile Information Sharing

We do not sell, share, rent, or disclose your mobile phone number or SMS consent information to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third party. Your mobile number is used solely for direct service communications related to your IT support and managed services with One I.T. Pro.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Access to client information is restricted to personnel who require it to deliver contracted services.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to deliver our services, maintain accurate support and service records, and satisfy applicable legal, tax, and regulatory obligations. Records associated with closed accounts are retained in accordance with our standard retention schedule and then securely disposed of.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The most current version will always be available at policiesandterms.com/oneitpro/privacy-policy.
