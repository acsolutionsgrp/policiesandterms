These Terms and Conditions govern your participation in SMS messaging communications with Osorio Iron Works Inc, doing business as Osorio Metals Supply ("we," "us," or "our"). By providing your mobile number to our team or by texting us directly, you agree to these terms.

1. Program Description

Osorio Metals Supply operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with customers about quoting, ordering, and delivery. Messages may include:

  • Material quotes and pricing, sent as a secure document link
  • Order confirmations and material availability updates
  • Notifications that an order is ready for pickup or out for delivery
  • Delivery scheduling and jobsite coordination
  • Responses to customer inquiries
  • Account and invoice notices related to an existing order

2. Consent

By providing your mobile number to our sales team by phone, at our counter, on a quote request, or by texting Osorio Metals Supply directly, you agree to receive SMS messages related to your quotes, orders, and deliveries.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. You may buy material from Osorio Metals Supply regardless of whether you consent to SMS communications. Quotes remain available by email, by fax, and in person at our counter.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your quote requests, order activity, and interactions with our team. Most customers receive messages only when a quote is issued or an order changes status.

5. Opt-Out

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent, unless you re-initiate contact.

7. Quote and Document Links

Quotes are not sent as file attachments. We send a text message containing a link to a page on our own domain at quotes.osoriometals.com, where the quote can be viewed or downloaded on your phone.

Each link is unique to a single document, is not publicly listed, and expires automatically after a limited period. We do not use public URL shorteners, and we will never ask for a password, payment card number, or Social Security number by text message or through a linked page.

If a link does not open or has expired, reply to the message or call us. Our team can reissue the quote at no charge.
