Osorio Iron Works Inc, doing business as Osorio Metals Supply ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our customers and contacts. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information, including information collected via SMS/text messaging as part of our quoting, ordering, and delivery services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, company name, phone number, and email address
  • Billing, jobsite, and delivery addresses
  • Mobile phone numbers for SMS communication purposes
  • Quote, order, and delivery history records
  • Payment and account information as required for billing purposes
  • Communication records including SMS message history

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Send material quotes and pricing as a secure document link
  • Confirm orders, material availability, and lead times
  • Notify customers when an order is ready for pickup or out for delivery
  • Coordinate delivery scheduling and jobsite access
  • Respond to customer inquiries and service requests
  • Comply with applicable legal, tax, and recordkeeping requirements

3. SMS Messaging

By providing your mobile number to our sales team by phone, at our counter, or by texting us directly, you consent to receive SMS messages from Osorio Metals Supply related to your quotes, orders, and deliveries. Message frequency varies based on your order activity.

Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP. Message frequency varies.

4. Quote and Document Links

Quotes and related documents are not sent as file attachments. Instead, we send a text message containing a link to a page hosted on our own domain at quotes.osoriometals.com, where the document can be viewed or downloaded.

Each link is unique to a single document and recipient, is not publicly listed or indexed, and expires automatically after a limited period. We record when a link is created, to which number it was sent, and when it is first opened, solely to confirm delivery and to provide customer service.

We do not use public URL shorteners. Every link we send resolves directly to a page on a domain owned and operated by Osorio Metals Supply. We never ask for passwords, payment card numbers, or Social Security numbers through a text message or a linked page.

5. No Mobile Information Sharing

We do not sell, share, rent, or disclose your mobile phone number or SMS consent information to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third party. Your mobile number is used solely for direct business communications related to your quotes, orders, and deliveries with Osorio Metals Supply.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal and business information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Document links are served over encrypted HTTPS connections and expire on a set schedule.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The most current version will always be available at policiesandterms.com/osoriometals/privacy-policy. Continued use of our SMS services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
