How customers opt in
Verbal consent, given in person at our counter or by phone
There is no web form, paper form, QR code, or text keyword opt-in for this program.

Opt-in workflow

1
A customer calls (773) 772-4060 or visits our counter at 4518 W Haddon Ave, Chicago, IL 60651 to request a price quote on metal products.
2
After taking the quote request, our staff member asks the customer, verbatim:
"Would you like us to text you your quote and order updates? You will only receive messages about your quotes, orders, and deliveries. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Our Terms are at policiesandterms.com/osoriometals/terms/ and our Privacy Policy is at policiesandterms.com/osoriometals/privacy-policy/. Do I have your permission to text you at this mobile number?"
3
The customer must verbally answer yes. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Customers who decline still receive their quote by email, by fax, or in person at our counter.
4
The staff member records the customer name, mobile number, date, and staff initials against the customer account in our system.
5
We then send a one-time confirmation text:
Osorio Metals Supply: You're all set. You'll now receive quotes and order updates from our team. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
6
Quotes are delivered as a text message containing a link to a page on quotes.osoriometals.com, a domain owned and operated by Osorio Iron Works Inc. We do not use public URL shorteners.
